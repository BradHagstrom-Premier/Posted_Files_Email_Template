# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Definian consultants running Oracle Cloud conversion engagements. Starting with the AWC project (Brad Hagstrom, Fyzia), expanding to all Definian client projects. Users are experienced professionals who open this tool to produce a specific deliverable quickly and move on. They are not experimenting; they know what they need.

## Product Purpose

A browser-based email generator that produces properly formatted, Outlook-compatible email notifications for Oracle Cloud conversion cycles. Two modes: Posted Files (pre-load notifications to stakeholders) and Recon Report (post-load validation results). The tool eliminates manual formatting errors and ensures every consultant produces on-brand, structurally consistent emails regardless of project or client.

## Brand Personality

Precise, no-nonsense, professional. The tool is a reflection of Definian's consulting quality — confident and competent, not decorative. It respects the user's time and does not call attention to itself.

## Anti-references

- Flashy SaaS marketing tools: over-designed, startup aesthetic, gradient-heavy, animated for animation's sake
- Generic SharePoint / enterprise portal UIs: clunky, gray, zero visual investment
- Tools that feel like prototypes or internal hacks rather than polished products

## Design Principles

1. **Get out of the way.** The interface exists to serve the output. No element should require interpretation or slow the user down.
2. **Represent the brand.** This tool will be used in front of clients, shared across engagements, and is itself a signal of Definian quality. It must look intentional.
3. **Consistency is the feature.** Uniform email output across consultants and projects is the entire point. The UI should reinforce that precision.
4. **Clarity over cleverness.** Labels, states, and copy should be immediately obvious. No learning curve for new consultants picking it up on a new project.
5. **Respect the Outlook constraint.** The output layer (email HTML) is non-negotiable. The generator UI can be modern; the email output cannot. Keep those concerns clearly separated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. No known specific user needs. Keyboard navigability expected for a professional tool.
